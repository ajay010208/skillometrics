## Slide 2 — IDEA TITLE / PROPOSED SOLUTION

**Deck title (already applied):** SkilloMetrics — AI Career & Skilling Outcome Platform

> The deck carries a condensed 6-bullet version of this; this file is the extended source. Keep the template's pointer headings.

### Detailed explanation of the proposed solution
- One platform connecting the whole skilling chain: trainees, recruiters, training providers, government
- Trainee path: consent-based profile → resume parsing → AI skill analysis with gaps mapped to free courses
- Honest "Reality Check": employability verdict (e.g. 68% ready — NOT YET, 2/6 requirements met) + weeks-to-employable estimate
- Week-by-week roadmap of free resources (freeCodeCamp, NPTEL, Microsoft Learn, YouTube) with duration, prerequisites, cost
- AI quizzes + project validation update skill levels → readiness recalculates live
- Job matching with explainable scores; recruiter pipeline from shortlist → interview → hire
- Hire auto-triggers placement record + 3/6/12-month follow-ups + verified alumni review prompt
- Government dashboard aggregates everything: placement %, retention curve, wage progression, gap analytics

### How it addresses the problem
- Skilling today ends at certificates: nobody tracks whether training led to a job that lasted
- SkilloMetrics adds the missing outcome layer — verified placement, retention, wage growth
- Trainees stop spending on outcome-less courses; the market gets evidence, not claims
- Consent-based data builds trust: trainees opt in and can audit access
- Government finally sees which programs work — per state, per scheme, per skill

### Innovation and uniqueness
- 3/6/12-month longitudinal follow-ups — no mainstream skilling platform does this
- Verified outcomes: employer-confirmed hires + trainee cross-checks, not self-report
- Explainable AI: every match and verdict shows its reasoning ("why 82%?")
- Honest verdicts: "NOT YET" always comes with a free path forward — dignity plus direction
- Never-breaks demo: deterministic AI fallbacks when no LLM key is set; same architecture scales from laptop to nation
